Connect your charger

Connect your charger and point your charger's OCPP client at this Homey:
  1. Open your charger's OCPP settings.
  2. Set the URL to the address below.
    ws://…
  3. Leave Charge point identity as whatever your charger already filled in (or set your own).
  4. Use OCPP 1.6J over WebSocket.
  5. Save and let the charger connect, then continue.
