Connect your charger

Connect your charger and point your charger's OCPP client at this Homey:

  1. Open your charger's OCPP settings.
  2. Set the OCPP URL to the address below.
  3. Use OCPP 1.6J over WebSocket. Leave the charge point identity as is.
  4. Save and let the charger connect, then continue.
